## Starting with a system

I did not treat 875 TOEIC as a goal that would happen by simply trying harder. For me, it was a system problem: understand where I was weak, practice in short loops, measure often, and adjust the method when the data showed it was not working.

## How I studied

I split the work into two main areas: improving natural listening comprehension and reading under time pressure. Each study session had a small goal so the process never felt too vague.

- Listening: hear phrases, record mistakes, and replay difficult parts.
- Reading: improve speed, spot traps, and manage time.
- Review: group mistakes by pattern instead of fixing questions one by one.

## What kept me going

The score did not come from one intense week. It came from ordinary days: opening the material, doing one small section, writing down mistakes, and returning the next day.

This journey made me trust structured self-learning more. When the goal is clear and feedback is fast, studying feels less vague, less discouraging, and much easier to sustain.
