## Leaving the usual pace

Bu Gia Map stayed with me because it felt completely different from my normal rhythm. No screens, no notifications, no pressure to process everything quickly. Just forest trails, footsteps, streams, and climbs that forced me to stay present.

## On the trail

Trekking reminded me of simple things that are easy to forget: moving fast is not always better, saving energy matters more than showing off, and a group that keeps the same rhythm makes the whole journey easier.

- Pack light, useful, and easy to reach.
- Keep a steady pace instead of burning out early.
- Watch the weather, terrain, and energy of the group.

## After the trip

Bu Gia Map made persistence feel more concrete. The same is true at work: difficult problems are rarely solved by sprinting once. They are usually solved by continuing, checking direction, and adjusting a little at a time.

This trip was more than a trek. It felt like a reset that helped me return to code and product work with a calmer mind.
